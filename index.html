How it works

Transparent checks, local processing, and clear limitations.

STEP 01
Input and parsing

Paste email text or load a sanitised .eml/.txt file. Python's standard email parser extracts available headers and readable text parts.

STEP 02
Indicator checks

Documented rules inspect display-name mismatches, URL patterns, urgency language, sensitive-information requests, and supplied authentication results.

STEP 03
Risk summary

Each indicator contributes a visible number of points. The total is capped at 100 and grouped into Low, Moderate, High, or Very high.

STEP 04
Learn and verify

Review evidence and advice, then use the awareness quiz. No link is opened and no remote content is fetched.

Scoring guide

Current rule weights are defined in analyzer.py and can be adjusted and tested.

Risk scoreLevelInterpretation
0–24LowFew or no configured warning signals were detected.
25–49ModerateReview the listed signals and verify independently.
50–74HighSeveral or stronger warning signals were detected.
75–100Very highMultiple strong signals were detected; handle with caution.

Weights are teaching heuristics, not calibrated probabilities. A score of 92 does not mean a 92% probability of phishing.
